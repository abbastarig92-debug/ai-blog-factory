## Technical Guide: Optimizing Prompts for 9:16 Vertical Video

Generating vertical video requires a different prompting strategy than widescreen. If you do not specify the framing, the AI will often generate a squished horizontal perspective.

*   **Use vertical framing keywords:** Include phrases like "vertical cinematic shot," "9:16 portrait framing," and "full-body vertical shot" at the start of your prompt. This forces the AI to utilize the height of the frame.
*   **Specify vertical camera movements:** Prompts that specify "slow vertical tilt up," "crane shot descending," or "push forward zoom" translate beautifully to mobile screens. Avoid wide pans, which often create empty space at the top and bottom of a vertical render.
*   **Avoid horizontal bias:** Do not prompt wide landscapes or sprawling crowds. Keep the focus on a single subject, such as "a close-up portrait of a chef slicing vegetables, vertical frame."

### Can I use free AI-generated videos for commercial Instagram Reels?

### How do I remove an AI watermark without losing video quality?

The industry standard for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts is portrait (9:16) [5]. Using this native setting ensures your video fills the entire mobile screen without black bars on the top and bottom.
