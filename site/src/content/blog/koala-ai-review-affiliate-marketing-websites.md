## Fact-Checking and Content Polish: Where Koala AI Falls Short

While Koala AI speeds up raw text generation, publishing output without manual editing creates quality risks for your site.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                   EDITORIAL POLISH CHECKLIST                          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [ ] Verify current price points against live Amazon listing pages     |
| [ ] Check technical specifications (dimensions, materials, weight)    |
| [ ] Remove repetitive transition phrases ("In conclusion", "Overall")  |
| [ ] Add original product testing imagery for Google E-E-A-T compliance|
| [ ] Confirm Amazon tracking tag presence on custom call-to-action links|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Pricing and Availability Drift

Amazon sellers adjust pricing and stock levels constantly. Even though Koala fetches live data at write-time, price listings can become outdated after publishing.

Writing exact dollar amounts into static article body text creates accuracy issues down the road. Editors should modify generated text to use price indicators (e.g., "$", "$$", "$$$") rather than fixed dollar values, relying on dynamic Amazon API widgets for exact real-time pricing.

### Repetitive Structural Phrasing

Unedited Koala output tends to adopt predictable linguistic habits. The AI frequently starts pros and cons lists with identical sentence structures and relies on cliché conclusions like "Overall, this product offers great value for the money."

Running a quick manual edit to vary sentence openings, trim fluff, and align tone with your brand voice prevents your site from sounding repetitive.

### Satisfying Google E-E-A-T Guidelines

Search engine quality guidelines reward evidence of real-world product testing. Koala AI synthesizes web data, but it cannot replace first-hand experience.

To rank competitive commercial terms long-term, editors can supplement Koala drafts with custom editorial insights:
1. Replace stock product descriptions with original hands-on testing feedback.
2. Insert custom original photographs or video embeds showing the item in use.
3. Add specific testing methodology notes detailing how products were evaluated.
