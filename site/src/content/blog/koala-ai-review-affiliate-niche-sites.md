## SERP Retrieval Accuracy: Does Koala Actually Research?

The "Use Real-Time Search Results" toggle allows Koala to access information beyond standard LLM training data. It pulls live Amazon data—including current pricing, star ratings, and review summaries—without requiring a personal Amazon Associates API key.

However, inaccuracies can still occur in technical specifications. Users have reported instances where the AI misidentifies specific features, such as armrest adjustments or material types, even when the live listing states otherwise. Technical tables should be verified rather than trusted blindly. You should check "load capacity" and "material" specs, as the AI may occasionally conflate data from similar models found in search results.

Koala’s source citations assist with verification. It lists the URLs crawled at the bottom of the draft, allowing editors to click through and double-check specific claims. This provides a more transparent workflow than standard manual prompting in general-purpose AI tools.

## Automated Internal Linking & SEO Optimization Features

The KoalaLinks integration allows you to connect a WordPress site so the AI can scan existing content. When generating roundups, it can insert links to previous relevant posts. These links are designed to be contextually accurate and placed within the flow of the text.

For SEO optimization, Koala uses entity analysis to suggest keywords based on search intent. It aims to weave in relevant industry terms found in current top-ranking SERP results rather than relying on simple keyword density.

**One consideration:** The automated internal linking may occasionally target non-informational pages like "Contact Us" if they are not excluded in the settings. Users should manage their dashboard settings to ensure links are directed toward relevant content pages.

## KoalaWriter vs. KoalaChat: Workflow Efficiency for Creators

KoalaWriter is designed for structured long-form content, while KoalaChat handles ancillary tasks. For content creators, KoalaChat can be used to adapt blog posts into different formats, such as video scripts or social media descriptions, by referencing the URL of a generated article.

The "Custom Outline" feature in KoalaWriter is useful for maintaining a specific brand voice. By using the outline editor, users can ensure the inclusion of specific sections, such as a "How We Tested" methodology or a niche-specific "Buyer’s Guide," rather than relying on a generic structure.

The Brand DNA feature allows users to define their audience, tone, and buying philosophy. This helps ensure that articles across a site maintain a consistent voice and adhere to the publisher's specific content standards.

## The Formatting Factor: From Koala to WordPress

Koala exports to WordPress using Gutenberg blocks. It generates standard header tags, bulleted lists for pros and cons, and comparison tables. This reduces the time required for manual formatting before publication.

The tool includes a customizable "Affiliate Disclosure" block to assist with disclosure requirements. While the "KoalaImages" feature can generate AI headers, some users may prefer using official product images pulled via the Amazon integration for a more traditional aesthetic.

**Technical Note:** The output uses native HTML. This is designed to work with major themes like GeneratePress or Astra without requiring significant CSS adjustments or cleaning up messy code wrappers.

This **koala ai review for affiliate niche sites** finds that Koala is a highly functional tool for affiliate publishers looking to increase their output. ### Frequently asked questions

### How much does a Koala AI article cost?
Costs vary by plan and model. On high-volume Scale plans, the cost per word is lower, while on entry-level plans, a single long-form article using advanced models can consume a significant portion of the monthly credit limit.

### Does Koala AI pass factual accuracy checks for product reviews?

Yes, via the KoalaLinks feature. By connecting a WordPress site, the tool can scan existing slugs to insert contextually relevant internal links. Users can blacklist specific pages to ensure links remain relevant to the topic.
