## [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/?via=abbas) vs HeyGen: Comparison for B2B Sales Teams

Choosing between **synthesia vs heygen for b2b sales videos** depends on whether you need a structured training tool or a personalization engine. HeyGen’s motion is often described as fluid for prospecting, while Synthesia’s slide-based editor is designed to simplify complex product walkthroughs.

| Feature / Capability | Synthesia | HeyGen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Use Case** | L&D and Sales Enablement | Cold Outreach & Marketing |
| **Stock Avatars** | 240+ | 700+ |
| **Max Resolution** | 1080p | 4K (Business plan and above) |
| **Editor Style** | Slide-deck (PowerPoint-like) | Creative Timeline |
| **Custom Avatar** | Paid add-on | Available on paid plans |
| **Language Support**| 140+ | 175+ |

HeyGen is often preferred for personalized outreach due to its creative flexibility. Synthesia is a strong choice for enterprise-wide sales enablement where brand consistency and structured templates are the priority.

## Avatar Realism & Prospect Engagement in Sales Outreach

HeyGen produces natural head movement and eye contact, which can be a fit for creators and small businesses building a personal brand. Some users find that HeyGen avatars show a range of expressions that help engagement during a cold pitch.

Synthesia takes a corporate approach. The avatars are steady and formal, which works well for internal training or FAQ videos. While Synthesia provides 1080p output, HeyGen offers 4K resolution on its higher tiers.

One potential downside to HeyGen is the possibility of rendering artifacts. Users have reported issues with hand and jewelry rendering, such as "warped fingers" or "hallucinated rings," even when source footage is clean. Synthesia’s avatars are generally more stable but may feel more formal if your script requires high energy.

## Custom Voice Cloning & Variable Personalization

HeyGen offers instant voice cloning for personal branding. Paid plans include a monthly credit allocation that users can spend across features. This model allows sales reps to use a cloned voice across various personalized videos.

Synthesia provides voice cloning and custom avatars, though these features may involve additional costs or specific plan requirements. This can make it a more significant investment for individual sales reps compared to HeyGen’s credit-based inclusion in paid plans.

Both platforms support dynamic data tags. You can insert a prospect’s name, company, or a specific industry into the script. HeyGen’s multilingual capabilities support over 175 languages compared to Synthesia’s 140+, which is a factor for global sales teams.

## CRM Integrations & Workflow Automation Pipelines

Both tools offer API access and Zapier integrations to automate video generation. For a B2B sales engine, you can trigger a video creation when a lead enters a CRM like HubSpot or Salesforce. HeyGen is often used for campaigns where you need to generate videos from a spreadsheet.

*   **HeyGen Workflow:** Trigger via Zapier → Pull prospect name from CRM → Generate video with custom avatar → Send via outreach tool.
*   **Synthesia Workflow:** Trigger via API → Update slide-based template → Export 1080p video → Sync data back to CRM.

Synthesia’s pricing is structured around annual video minutes or credits, which favors predictable usage. HeyGen’s credit system offers flexibility for fluctuating campaign volumes.

## Motion Design, Custom Branding, & Video Editing Features

The editing experience differs significantly between the two. Synthesia uses a PowerPoint-like interface. You build videos scene-by-scene as if you were making a deck. This is designed for speed for sales enablement teams who already have existing training materials.

HeyGen uses a timeline-based editor that functions more like traditional video editing software. This allows for creative motion graphics, screen recording overlays, and animated call-to-action (CTA) elements. If you need to overlay a prospect’s website behind your avatar, HeyGen’s creative studio is built for this execution.

Synthesia’s "AI Video Assistant" helps generate scripts and scenes. However, HeyGen’s 4K export option provides a level of resolution that Synthesia’s 1080p limit does not currently match.

## Pricing Models & Credit Consumption

Synthesia’s pricing starts at $29 per month for the Starter plan. If you pay annually, the price is approximately $18 per month. The Creator plan is $89 per month. Synthesia plans are structured around available minutes or credits, which requires planning for high-volume teams.

HeyGen’s Creator plan starts at $29 per month (or $24 billed annually). The Pro plan has been cited at various price points including $49, $79, or $99 per month depending on the billing cycle and credit requirements. For large sales teams, the Business plan starts at $149 per month.

## Final Decision Framework: Which AI Video Platform to Choose?

When deciding on **synthesia vs heygen for b2b sales videos**, the choice depends on your specific outreach strategy. If your goal is to send personalized videos to cold prospects, HeyGen’s credit model and motion style are competitive.

Choose HeyGen if:
*   You need high-volume personalization for cold outreach.
*   You want to use custom avatar features within a standard paid plan.
*   You require 4K resolution for marketing assets.

Choose Synthesia if:
*   You are building an internal sales training library.
*   You prefer a slide-based editor that mirrors PowerPoint.
*   You need enterprise-level structured templates.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which platform produces more convincing custom avatars for personalized sales prospecting?
HeyGen is often noted for producing convincing avatars for prospecting because of its natural head movement and eye contact. Synthesia’s avatars are highly realistic but tend to be more static, making them a common choice for formal training.

### How do HeyGen and Synthesia integrate with CRMs like HubSpot and Salesforce?
Both platforms integrate with major CRMs via Zapier and native APIs. You can set up a workflow so that when a new lead is created, a personalized video is generated and the link is recorded in the contact record.

### Can you dynamically generate personalized videos at scale using Zapier or APIs?
Yes, both tools support automated generation. HeyGen is frequently used for high-volume scale because its credit-based system and timeline editor are built for marketing automation, whereas Synthesia is optimized for structured, template-based content.

### Which platform offers better credit efficiency for high-volume cold outreach?
HeyGen is often cited for outreach efficiency because its paid plans include custom avatar features that may require additional fees on other platforms. HeyGen's credit model also allows for flexibility during high-volume campaign months.
