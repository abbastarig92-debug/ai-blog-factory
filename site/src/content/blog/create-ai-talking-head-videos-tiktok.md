## Scripting for TikTok Retention with AI Avatars

AI presenters do not have natural charisma by default. Your script structure must carry the retention curve.

### The Opening Hook

AI voices can sound flat if the sentence structure drags. Start immediately with a punchy statement or an abrupt question:

> *"Stop spending hours editing your vertical shorts."*

Do not let the avatar introduce itself, say hello, or ask how the viewer is doing. Viewers often decide whether to swipe almost immediately.

### The Infinite Loop Structure

To encourage TikTok's replay metrics, link the final sentence seamlessly back into your opening hook.

* **Closing sentence:** *"And that is the exact reason why you need to..."*
* **Opening hook:** *"...use dynamic motion design on every single video."*

When the video loops on the user's feed, the audio transition sounds continuous, encouraging the platform's retention calculation to register a repeated view.

### Pacing and Script Length

Keep your script pace moderate. If you cram too many words into your generation, the AI voice model will rush its syllables, creating awkward synthetic cadences and broken lip movements.

## Customizing the Visuals: Backgrounds and Framing

A raw AI avatar floating over an empty white void or a fake 3D office can signal low effort. Ground your avatar in a realistic setting using motion design basics.


### Ditch the AI Studio Presets

Export your avatar on a green screen or solid background, then bring the file into an editor. Place realistic vertical b-roll behind the avatar:
* A blurred desk setup with subtle background movement.
* Looping screen recordings of the product or workflow you are explaining.
* Mood lighting with soft out-of-focus highlights.

### Use the Dynamic Punch-In

Avoid letting an AI avatar sit at the same camera distance for too long without variation. Apply digital camera cuts inside your editing timeline:
* **Base Frame:** Standard scale, chest-up view.
* **Punch-In Frame:** A tight punch-in zoom on key transitions or emphasis words.
* **Slide Frame:** Shift the avatar to the side of the screen when bringing up an on-screen visual aid.

These hard cuts break visual monotony and help reset viewer attention.

## Post-Production: Adding Captions and B-Roll

Raw exports from AI platforms often benefit from a second pass in editing software before they are ready for TikTok.

* **Word-by-word animated captions:** Captions keep people watching even when they are scrolling with the sound off. Generate dynamic captions centered across the lower-middle portion of the video frame. Use high-contrast colors paired with a dark drop shadow.
* **Cover mouth glitches with visual overlays:** AI lip-sync occasionally blurs or stutters on unusual brand names or technical jargon. Mask these rendering issues by dropping relevant screenshots, diagrams, or stock footage over the timeline right when the phrase is spoken.
* **Blend background audio:** Add a trending sound directly inside the TikTok app before publishing. Keep the background music volume low while leaving your speech audio clear and audible. This lets you tap into search algorithms without drowning out the speech.

## Avoiding the Uncanny Valley: Production Rules

Synthetic visuals can degrade viewer trust quickly. Follow these production rules to keep your footage looking grounded:

1. **Avoid full-body generations:** Full-body AI models can look detached from natural motion. Their hands drift, torso movements look separated from their legs, and clothing textures stretch unnaturally. Sticking to a waist-up or chest-up crop works best.
2. **Lock the eye line:** In your generator's advanced settings, confirm the eye-contact toggle is active. If the avatar's pupils float upward or drift across the screen, the synthetic nature becomes immediately obvious.
3. **Insert manual pauses:** Natural human speech contains natural pauses and imperfect rhythm. Break up long script paragraphs using punctuation. Many voice generators also support `[pause]` tags—use these between major ideas.

## TikTok Policy and Disclosure for AI Content

TikTok actively moderates synthetic media. Navigating its community guidelines properly can help keep your content visible and help avoid distribution limits.

* **Toggle the AI disclosure setting:** Whenever you upload an avatar-led video, check the **"AI-generated content"** toggle in the upload options. TikTok requires this tag for realistic synthetic media.
* **Avoid misleading impersonation:** Do not clone a public figure’s likeness or voice without authorization. Use stock avatars provided by the software or clone yourself with proper verification.
* **Keep value high:** Applying manual edits, captions, and thoughtful scripting helps your clips pass standard quality reviews.
