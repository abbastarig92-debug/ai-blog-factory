## Core Criteria for the Best AI Video Generator for Employee Onboarding

When selecting an AI video generator for employee onboarding, look for three technical foundations: templates, overlays, and governance.

**Pre-built HR Templates**
HR teams shouldn't start with a blank timeline. Leading platforms offer templates for "Day 1 Agenda," "Benefits Overview," and "IT Security Protocols." These templates should include placeholders for your specific software screenshots and company-specific text.

**Technical Overlay Requirements**
Software onboarding requires showing the interface. You need a platform that allows you to record your screen directly in the browser and then "layer" the AI avatar over the corner of the footage. If you have to export a screen recording from another tool and then import it, your production time increases significantly.

**Governance and SSO**
For mid-market and enterprise companies, SAML 2.0 or Okta integration is often a key requirement. It allows you to manage permissions centrally. Ensure the tool meets enterprise security standards to protect the employee data you might include in training scripts.

## Avatar Integration vs. Motion Graphics: Structuring Engaging Training Modules

To keep viewers engaged, avoid letting an avatar talk for too long without a visual change. We recommend balancing screen time, showing the software UI or key bullet points alongside footage of the avatar.

*   **Sequence for Software Demos:** Start with the avatar introducing the tool, then transition to a full-screen recording with the avatar in a small overlay in the corner.
*   **Visual Hierarchy:** Use automated text highlights. If the avatar mentions a specific button, that button should be highlighted on the screen recording simultaneously.
*   **B-Roll Automation:** Some tools can automatically suggest stock footage based on your script keywords, breaking up the monotony of a single presenter.

## Cost and Maintenance Analysis: Script Updates vs. Re-Shoots

A primary advantage of AI video is simplified updates. In a traditional production, changing a single sentence about a company policy requires re-hiring the actor, setting up the studio, and re-editing the entire file.

**Standard Maintenance Workflow:**
1.  Open the existing project.
2.  Highlight the outdated sentence in the script.
3.  Type the new policy change.
4.  Hit "Generate."
5.  Re-export the SCORM package to the LMS.

This process takes minutes compared to a traditional multi-week production cycle. Most platforms charge based on credits or tiered plans. Synthesia’s Starter plan costs $29/month, while HeyGen’s Business plan starts at $149/month for teams needing 4K rendering and SSO.

## Implementation SOP: Launching Your First AI-Driven Onboarding Module

Follow this checklist to move from a raw PDF to a deployed training module:

1.  **Script Ingestion:** Upload your employee handbook or policy PDF to the AI tool to generate a first-draft script.
2.  **Avatar Selection:** Choose an avatar that matches your company culture. Use professional avatars for welcomes and casual avatars for peer-to-peer training.
3.  **Screen Capture:** Record your internal tools using the native screen recorder.
4.  **SSO Setup:** Connect the platform to your directory provider to grant your HR team access.
5.  **Brand Kit Application:** Apply your company colors and logos to the template.
6.  **LMS Export:** Export the final file as a SCORM or xAPI package. This ensures you can track viewing progress and quiz results.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which AI video generator has the best pre-built HR templates?
Synthesia offers a comprehensive library for HR, with templates specifically designed for onboarding, compliance, and enablement. These templates include pre-set layouts for company values and security awareness that allow you to adapt the text and generate.

### Can you record your screen and overlay an AI presenter?
Yes, platforms like Synthesia and HeyGen offer native screen recording. You can capture your browser or desktop and then position the AI avatar as a floating bubble or a side-by-side presenter, which works well for software walkthroughs and IT setup guides.

### Which platforms support enterprise SSO and SCORM export?
Synthesia and Colossyan are strong choices for LMS integration, offering native SCORM and xAPI exports. For security, Synthesia (Enterprise) and HeyGen (Business plan and up) support SAML 2.0 and Okta for centralized identity management.

### How do I update a video if a company policy changes?
You simply log into the platform, edit the text in the script for that specific scene, and click "Generate." The AI will re-render only the affected portion, allowing you to update a global policy video quickly without a re-shoot.
