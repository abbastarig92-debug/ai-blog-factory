### Custom Avatar Pricing Realities

While Synthesia offers standard stock avatars out of the box, building a custom personal avatar for a small business founder requires an annual plan commitment plus a separate add-on fee. If you want your founder or HR lead on screen, factor that additional cost into your software planning.

### Daily UI Bottlenecks for Non-Editors

Non-designers will appreciate the clean layout, but several constraints surface quickly:

* **Canvas positioning:** You work within pre-set bounding boxes and grid alignments. Layering complex graphical interactions feels clunky compared to dedicated timeline editors.
* **Scene transition caps:** Custom transition timings between complex multi-element slides remain limited.
* **Voice inflection control:** Fine-tuning emotional emphasis is restricted to pacing breaks and manual phonetic spellings.

Synthesia's pricing structure hinges on monthly and yearly video minute allowances:

* **Free Plan ($0):** Includes 10 minutes of video per month and basic features. All exports carry a visible watermark.
* **Starter Plan ($29/month billed monthly, or $18/month billed annually):** Includes 10 minutes of video per month, AI avatars, and removes the watermark.

## Video Quality & Avatar Naturalness: Where Synthesia Excels and Fails

Synthesia works best when **clarity and accuracy outweigh dramatic emotion**.

* **Micro-expressions and eye contact:** Stock avatars maintain direct, steady eye contact. Head turns and blinks look clean in formal corporate attire, though gestures can feel repetitive if an avatar speaks continuously for extended periods without B-roll overlays.
* **Voice synthesis & phonetic overrides:** Synthetic voices occasionally mispronounce company acronyms, technical terms, or non-English brand names. You can fix this directly in the script box by using phonetic spelling (e.g., spelling out *"SaaS"* as *"Sass"* or *"S-A-A-S"*).
* **High-stakes vs. low-stakes fit:** Synthesia is well-suited for compliance reviews, cyber-security policies, and internal SOPs. For public brand campaigns or emotive sales pitches, AI presenters can still feel a bit too rigid.

## Step-by-Step: Building an HR Onboarding Video in Synthesia

Follow this workflow to create a standard onboarding module without design experience:

### 1. Document Ingestion
Click **New Video** and upload your raw employee handbook chapter or slide deck. The AI Video Assistant can generate draft scenes with paired narration.

### 2. Scene Staging & Avatar Selection
Choose an avatar dressed to match your company's culture. Position the avatar on the left or right third of the frame to leave space for media callouts.

### 3. Apply Brand Assets & Captions
Upload your company logo and brand color palette. Turn on **Auto-Captions**—crucial for desk employees who watch internal videos with the sound off.

### 4. Audio & Phonetic Polishing
Play the script audio preview. If an internal tool name or acronym sounds unnatural, adjust the script with phonetic spelling. Insert short pause markers between distinct bullet points.

### 5. Export and Deployment
Click **Generate**. Once rendering finishes, download the 1080p MP4 file, copy the hosted direct link, or embed the video inside your internal knowledge base.
