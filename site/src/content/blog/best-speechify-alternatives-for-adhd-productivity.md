## The ADHD Evaluation Matrix: Features That Actually Regulate Focus

### Word-by-Word Text Highlighting Sync
Auditory-visual drift breaks focus. When reading along with TTS, your visual cortex expects the highlighted word to illuminate the exact millisecond the phoneme hits your ears. 

If the highlight stutters or jumps whole paragraphs, your brain spends working memory trying to relocate your place on the page. Accurate word-level highlighting keeps your eyes anchored, preventing your mind from wandering off the page.

### Granular Speed Controls
Fixed speed jumps fail neurodivergent users. Your dopamine levels and processing bandwidth fluctuate throughout the day. 

You need fine speed adjustments. Slight incremental speed changes let you push past low-dopamine boredom without crossing the threshold into unintelligible chatter.

```
ADHD Speed Dialing:
[Slower Speeds] Proofreading copy & catching missing typos
[Moderate Speeds] Standard document consumption & research
[Higher Speeds] High-speed scanning for key data points
```

### Floating Mobile Widgets and Capture Overlays
Tab-switching triggers executive freeze. When listening to an article requires copying text, opening a secondary app, and clicking paste, the friction kills momentum. 

System-wide floating widgets let you select text in any app, hit a shortcut, and hear instant playback. Keeping the control overlay lightweight prevents visual clutter from taking over your workspace.

## Workflow Integration: Using TTS to Beat Creator Executive Dysfunction

### Bypassing the Blank Page
When executive dysfunction strikes, typing out an outline feels impossible. Use dictation tools like LumeVoice to record your raw thoughts out loud. 

Once your spoken words are converted to text, load that draft into NaturalReader at a faster playback speed. Hearing your draft read back to you immediately highlights pacing errors and missing transitions.

### Multi-Modal Proofreading Setup
Catching dropped words, duplicate verbs, and weird phrasing requires engaging two senses simultaneously. 

```
Proofreading Stack:
1. Open document in NaturalReader or Readwise Reader.
2. Set voice playback speed to a comfortable rate.
3. Keep eyes locked on highlighted words while ears listen.
4. Stop immediately when your ear hears a rhythm gap your eyes missed.
```

This dual-input approach catches subtle writing errors that standard spellcheckers skip right over.

### Keyboard-Driven Reading Workflows
Mouse navigation breaks focus. Searching for micro playback buttons on screen disrupts your hyperfocus state. Build a keyboard-first workflow instead:

*   `Spacebar` / `Option + S`: Instant Play / Pause toggle.
*   `Left / Right Arrows`: Skip backward/forward in short increments.
*   `Up / Down Arrows`: Adjust speed incrementally on the fly.
