Synthesia's custom digital twins capture fine physical gestures. When comparing **synthesia vs hour one for virtual brand ambassadors**, select the tool that aligns with your campaign architecture:

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use Synthesia and Hour One avatars in paid advertising campaigns?
Ad distribution policies depend on whether you use stock avatars or custom digital twins. Platforms may have specific guidelines regarding the use of stock models for promotional content. For paid campaigns, organizations often utilize custom avatars created with explicit spokesperson consent.

### How much does it cost to create a custom AI avatar of our own brand spokesperson on both platforms?
Synthesia offers personal avatars on select plans, such as the Pro tier. Hour One supports avatar customization, with pricing and options varying based on the selected package or enterprise consultation.

### Which platform has better integration for automated video generation pipelines?
Synthesia provides features for structured video production, including interactive videos and localization tools. Hour One focuses its workflows on text-to-video generation and template-based creation to streamline production for business users.

### Are there any distribution restrictions on the videos generated by these platforms?
Free-tier videos on both platforms typically include visual watermarks. Paid tiers generally offer additional rights, such as logo removal or commercial distribution, while enterprise plans provide custom licensing options tailored to organizational needs.
