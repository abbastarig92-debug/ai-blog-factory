## Feature Breakdown: What Real Estate Video Pipelines Actually Require

### Background Compositing
Real estate videos require placing the avatar over property footage. You need a timeline that supports layered tracks, allowing you to place drone footage on the bottom layer and the avatar (using green screen alpha keys or circular masks) on top.

### Script Generation Accuracy
Listing scripts must remain compliant with local advertising guidelines. The built-in AI writing assistants must accept specific instructions regarding fair housing rules and clear brokerage disclosures.

### Render Speeds and Aspect Ratios
Social platforms require 9:16 vertical layouts, while MLS portals and YouTube need 16:9 widescreen. The best pipelines export both formats in 1080p or 4K MP4 formats without needing external cropping tools.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up an Automated Real Estate Video Pipeline

### Step 1: Script Creation
Input your raw property description or URL into the AI script assistant. Set the tone to professional and direct, and add a clear call-to-action hook at the end.

### Step 2: Background Layering
Upload your high-resolution listing photos and 4K B-roll. Place them on the background track, and apply transitions between slides to keep the viewer engaged.

### Step 3: Avatar Setup & Export
Position your digital clone in the lower corner or center of the frame. Use the pronunciation tool to correct any misspelled street names or numbers, then click export to generate your MP4.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can AI avatars accurately pronounce specific street names, neighborhood jargon, and MLS numbers?
Yes, but you must use the platform's phonetic spelling or pronunciation tool. For example, spelling out "Tchoupitoulas" phonetically ensures the voice engine does not stumble during the audio render.

### How do you replace the AI avatar background with actual property walkthrough B-roll or drone video?
You upload your MP4 B-roll to the platform's media library, drag it onto the background layer of the timeline, and set your avatar's position to "overlay" or "picture-in-picture" on the top layer.

### What are the disclosure and licensing practices when using AI avatars in real estate ads?
Platforms like Argil require a consent video to train a clone, and industry standards often involve disclosing that the presenter is an AI-generated likeness depending on local guidelines.
