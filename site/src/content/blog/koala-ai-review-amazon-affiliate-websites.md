## How Koala AI Handles Amazon Roundup Creation (Step-by-Step Workflow)

Creating an affiliate roundup in Koala Writer starts with choosing your article type and selecting your AI engine, such as GPT-4o. You input your target keyword, enter your Amazon Associate Tag, and configure your direct link settings so calls-to-action automatically embed tracking parameters.

You can let Koala fetch products via automated live Amazon search or manually paste specific Amazon Standard Identification Numbers (ASINs). Manual ASIN input gives you tighter control over product selection, helping to prevent the tool from picking out-of-stock or low-rated items.

The tool builds H2 and H3 subheadings for each product, generates draft titles, and inserts product images directly into the layout. Call-to-action buttons are inserted right beneath each summary box to drive readers toward Amazon listings.

## Data Accuracy & Hallucination Audit: Specs, Pricing, and Reviews

KoalaWriter taps directly into Amazon data to retrieve product details, current prices, and customer reviews. This real-time fetch improves context, but human editorial oversight remains necessary.

Our audit showed that while text flows fluently, product details can still be wrong or generic. Dimensional specs, wattage ratings, and box inclusions occasionally drift into hallucinations. You should manually verify technical specs against the live seller listing before publishing.

For customer reviews, Koala extracts recurring pros and cons to form sentiment summaries. It handles Amazon dynamic pricing policies by steering clear of hardcoded static price claims. This aligns with Amazon's Product Advertising API (PA-API 5.0) requirements, which suggest avoiding static price displays that do not update via official API feeds.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Koala AI comply with Amazon Associates Operating Agreement regarding dynamic price displays?
Koala avoids hardcoding fixed prices directly into static text paragraphs, which can help publishers stay compliant with Amazon rules. However, you should still connect your official Amazon PA-API details or link widgets to handle real-time price updates dynamically.

### How accurate is Koala AI's automated ASIN and product specification extraction?
While Koala pulls data directly from Amazon, fluent text can still contain inaccurate or generic product details. Technical specs, dimensions, and feature lists require manual human verification before you publish the page.

### What is the exact cost per 2,000-word Amazon affiliate roundup article?
The cost depends on your subscription tier. On the $49 per month plan (20 articles), it costs $2.45 per article. On the $350 per month Elite plan (1,000,000 words), the cost is approximately $0.70 per article. Premium AI model usage consumes credits at 2x to 3x rates.

### Can Koala AI articles rank on Google without manual editing?
Koala articles can index and gain impressions, but unedited AI output often yields results that are good but not great. To sustain positions, an editor should refine the tone, verify product specs, and add original analysis to avoid potential thin-content penalties.
